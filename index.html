HOW TO PLAY

Desktop: W A S D move · Mouse aims · Left click shoots · SPACE launches missile · ESC pauses.
Mobile: left joystick moves (turret auto-aims at visible enemies) · hold FIRE to shoot · tap the missile button (60 s base reload).
Bullets: 10 dmg. Missile: 35 direct + 15 splash (65 px radius). Walls block everything. No friendly fire. Eliminate every enemy tank to win.
Battle Royale (ranked): 10 tanks, last one alive wins. The purple storm shrinks and burns anything outside it. 1st = +2 stars, top 3 = +1, top 5 = 0, below = −1 (quitting counts as a loss). Higher rank = smarter bots. Coins for placement and kills.
